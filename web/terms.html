What this site is

NBA Forecasts is a free, personal project by Noé Villarreal. It publishes statistical forecasts for NBA games and players and scores them against the real results. There are no accounts and nothing is sold.

Forecasts are estimates

Every number on this site comes from a statistical model and can be wrong, sometimes by a lot. The 80% ranges mean that about 2 in 10 actual values are expected to fall outside them. Use the forecasts for information and entertainment. They aren't betting, financial or professional advice.

Data sources

Historical statistics come from the Kaggle dataset Historical NBA Data and Player Box Scores. Current statistics come from the NBA stats API, accessed through the open-source nba_api library.
