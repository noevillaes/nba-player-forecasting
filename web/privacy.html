Privacy Policy

The short version

NBA Forecasts doesn't ask for, collect or store personal information about its visitors. There are no accounts, forms, cookies, analytics, ads or tracking scripts on this site.

What happens when you visit

The site is a set of static files hosted on GitHub Pages. Your browser downloads those files, including the forecast data and the Archivo typeface, directly from the same site. No other services are contacted.

GitHub, which hosts the site, may log technical information such as your IP address for security purposes, as described in the GitHub General Privacy Statement. The owner of NBA Forecasts doesn't have access to those logs. Draft: confirm against GitHub Pages' current documentation.

Your selections

The date, game, team and view you pick are saved in the page address after the # sign, so you can bookmark or share them. Browsers don't send that part of the address to the server. Nothing is saved on your device.

Information shown on the site

The site shows statistics and forecasts about professional NBA players and teams. The statistics come from public sources: the Kaggle dataset Historical NBA Data and Player Box Scores and the NBA stats API. The forecasts are produced by a statistical model from those statistics.

Children

The site doesn't collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If the site's data practices change, this page will be updated and the date at the top will change with it.
